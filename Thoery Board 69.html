Suraj Prasad

Founder, World of Thinker

Suraj started World of Thinker after noticing how many good ideas never get said out loud simply because they aren't "finished" or provable yet. A lifelong collector of odd hunches — about history, human behavior, and the occasional conspiracy about pigeons — Suraj wanted a home for the theories people usually keep to themselves.

"You don't need a PhD to have a theory. You just need to be curious enough to say it out loud."

Suraj believes the best ideas often start out sounding a little ridiculous, and that a community built around curiosity — not certainty — is worth building.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account info: username and password (stored as a hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile info: display name, bio, and profile picture, if you add them.
  • Content you post: theories, topics, and votes.
  • Preferences: theme choice (light/dark) and your cookie banner choice.

2. What's Public vs. Private

Your theories, votes, display name, avatar, and bio are visible to anyone using the board — that's the point of a shared theory board. Your password and personal preferences (like theme) are not shown to other users.

3. How We Use It

Your info is used to run your account, show your posts and profile to others, and remember your preferences between visits. We don't use it for anything beyond making the board work.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored using the board's built-in storage system, tied to your account. As noted at sign-up, this is a lightweight identity system for this board only — not enterprise-grade security. Don't reuse a sensitive password here.

5. Third Parties

We don't sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

6. Cookies & Storage

We use cookies and local storage for essential functions like staying logged in, and for optional preferences like theme. You can accept or decline non-essential storage from the banner shown on your first visit.

7. Your Choices

You can edit or delete your own theories and update your profile (name, bio, picture) at any time from My Profile.

8. Children's Privacy

World of Thinker isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. Continuing to use the board after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Why this place exists

A small mountain for your unproven ideas

Most of us are sitting on a theory we've never said out loud — about a show, a person, a pattern in the world, or something we can't quite explain. This board exists as a place to put those ideas down: no peer review, no proof required, just a space for hunches, hypotheses, and "hear me out" moments.
